Delete your account

Applies to the PuppyPulse app for Android. Last updated: November 6, 2026.

You can delete your PuppyPulse account and the data you own at any time. There are two ways to do it: inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open PuppyPulse and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

The app removes your account and the data you own. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: by email

Email contact@puppypulse.xyz with the subject "Delete my PuppyPulse account". Send it from the email address you use to sign in, so we can match the request to the account. If you signed in with Google and cannot send from that address, tell us the address and we will verify another way.

We will confirm by email when the deletion is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record.
  • Your personal profile information, such as your name and email address.
  • The data you own, including puppy profiles, care logs, tasks, reminders, health records with their attachments, milestones, training progress and progression entries, where you are the owner of that household.
  • Photos and attachments you uploaded, where they can be removed.
  • Your device notification token.

What may remain

  • Data in a shared household that belongs to the household rather than to you, such as care logs other members still see. That data stays so their records are not broken.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, which they keep under their own policies and legal obligations. Ask them directly if you also want those removed.
  • Crash reports and analytics events already collected, kept under the provider's standard retention settings.

For the full picture of what PuppyPulse collects and why, see the privacy policy.
