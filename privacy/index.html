Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

PuppyPulse is a puppy care tracking app for new puppy parents. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can remove it. PuppyPulse is a tracking tool, not veterinary advice.

PuppyPulse is operated by Kalamari, Canberra, Australian Capital Territory, Australia. Kalamari is the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at contact@puppypulse.xyz.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a user identifier through Firebase Authentication. We use this to identify you and connect you to your household.
  • Care data you enter. This includes your household name and member roles, puppy profiles (name, breed, sex, birthday, weight, microchip number, and any note you add), care events you log (for example potty, meals, naps, walks, weight, and their times and notes), reminders, care tasks and their assignments, health records, milestones and their completion dates, training progress, day plan preferences, progression photo entries (the stage, the capture date and any note), and custom activity definitions. This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (region australia-southeast1) and is visible to the members of your household.
  • Device tokens. A Firebase Cloud Messaging token is stored for your account so household notifications (for example "your partner logged a meal") can reach your device. Tokens are replaced when they change and are not used for advertising.
  • Purchase information. If you buy Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your purchase and entitlement status for a one-time unlock; we never see or store your card details.
  • Diagnostics. Firebase Analytics records basic usage events (such as first open, household created, puppy created, and purchases) and Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports with device model, OS version and app version. These help us keep the app working. We do not collect the advertising identifier.

Photos, attachments, and what stays on your device

  • Photos you attach to your puppy, to a care event, or to a progression stage are stored only on your device by default. The app keeps a file path, so other household members can see that an entry or stage exists but not the picture.
  • Cloud photo sync is an optional Premium feature that is off by default. When you turn it on, event photos are uploaded to Cloudflare R2 so everyone in your household can see them. You can turn it off again in Settings.
  • Progression photos are never uploaded. Only the stage, the capture date and any note you add are stored in Firestore.
  • When you attach a file to a health record, that file is uploaded to Cloudflare R2 (our storage provider) so everyone in your household can open it. Attachments are readable only by household members and are deleted when you remove the record or the attachment.
  • Small preferences (dark mode, widget appearance, weight unit, the puppy selected on this device, celebration effects, and whether photo sync is on) are stored locally on the device.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.
  • We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence models.

Service providers

We rely on: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, notifications, analytics, crash reporting), Google Play (payments), RevenueCat (purchase management), and Cloudflare (delivering household push notifications and storing photos and attachments in R2). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies, and some of them process data outside Australia.

Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account inside the app: Settings, then Delete account. This removes your account and the data you own, including your stored photos and attachments where it can.
  • Data that belongs to a shared household (for example care logs you added) may remain for the other household members.
  • You can also email us to request deletion, and we will remove your account and personal information. The steps are on the delete your account page.
  • Crash reports and analytics events are retained under the provider's standard retention settings.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access to a household's data is limited to its signed-in members by server-side security rules.

Children

PuppyPulse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact@puppypulse.xyz
