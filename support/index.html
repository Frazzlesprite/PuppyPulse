Support

Email contact@puppypulse.xyz and a person will read it. Tell us what happened, which phone you use, and the app version from Settings, About PuppyPulse if you can; it makes fixes much faster.

Getting started

Signing in

PuppyPulse uses Google Sign-In. If the sign-in sheet closes without finishing, check that Google Play services is up to date and that the phone has a working connection, then try again.

Inviting your household

Open Settings, Household, then Create invite code and share the code. It works once, when the other person signs in, and expires after seven days. Create a fresh code if it expires.

Adding a puppy

Settings, Pets, then Add a pet. The free tier covers one puppy; adding more is part of Premium.

Notifications and reminders

Reminders are not arriving

Check Settings, Notifications for the permission state and send a test. Android may also delay alarms in battery-saving modes; if reminders are consistently late, exempt PuppyPulse from battery optimisation in the phone's settings. Reminder timing is deliberately approximate to the minute, so a reminder can arrive a minute or so after the time you set.

My partner is not getting updates

Household updates need both people signed in on their own devices and in the same household. Local reminders are per device by design: each phone schedules its own.

Tapping a task notification

A due-task notification opens the Tasks screen and highlights the task it is about. Other notifications open the app on Today.

Sync and the household timeline

Changes sync live. If an entry looks missing, pull the app to the foreground and check the day selector: the Today screen can be showing a past day, which is labelled "Logged on" above the timeline.

Photos

Photos on another phone

Pet, event and progression photos stay on the device that took them by default. Event photos can be shared with the household by turning on Settings, Sync photos to the cloud, which is Premium and off by default. Health attachments are shared as soon as you attach them.

Replacing a missing photo

If a progression stage says its photo is missing on this device, open the stage and replace it. The stage itself and its date are still there.

The home screen widget

Add it from Settings, Home screen widget. If a pinned widget says it cannot load after an update, open PuppyPulse once; it refreshes the widget on launch. Widget appearance offers Default, Glass, Light and Dark.

Premium and purchases

Is it a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks features on the account that buys it. Other household members keep the free tier unless they unlock it too.

Restoring a purchase

Open the Premium screen and choose Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google account that bought it. If it still does not appear, email us with the Google account's email address (not a password).

Refunds

Google Play handles payments and refunds under its own policy. You can request a refund from your Play purchase history, or email us and we will point you to the right place.

Your data

Exporting

Settings, Export my data (Premium) produces CSVs and a vet-ready PDF. You can choose which sections to include and a date range for the care log.

Deleting your account

Settings, Delete account. See Delete your account for what is removed, what stays with the rest of the household, and how to ask us to do it for you.
